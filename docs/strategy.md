# Kurongeka: the business

_Prepared 25 September 2026. All prices in GBP._

## What we found

Kurongeka has been six different things in two years. None of them has earned money yet.

| When | What Kurongeka was | Where it lives | Outcome |
|---|---|---|---|
| Jun 2024 | Wix site "Kurongeka", Instagram @kurongekadotcom, LinkedIn page | Wix `Kurongeka` (ZA) | Empty store, free plan |
| Sep 2025 | "Africa's first engineered e-commerce platform", a B2B ERP beating SAP and Odoo | Drive: *Introduction to kurongeka.com*; Wix `kurongeka.com` | Every Wix app installed, no content; Premium lapsed Nov 2025 (card declined) |
| Sep 2025 | Service matrix: Shopps (shopping-centre systems), Konnect (SME digital transformation, local news, delivery, property, compliance) | Drive: *kurongeka.com.md*; Arundel Village proposal | Proposal only |
| Dec 2025 | Kurongeka Personal Ops: an ADHD productivity template in three tiers | Drive: *Yes* | Sales copy only |
| Apr–May 2026 | £350 brand audit, then a client cockpit, template store, supplier micro-sites and a patent log | GitHub `kurongeka` (live on Vercel), `kurongeka.com` (single HTML file) | Live, but with demo data presented as real and no leads; Supabase paused |
| Jul 2026 | Kurongeka OS: an internal command centre for Rodney's own sites | GitHub `kurongeka-os`, Vercel `kurongeka-os` | Internal tool |

The pattern is clear. Every version tried to be a platform before it had a single paying customer. Meanwhile, the thing Kapture already does well (launch small-business sites on Wix, fast, with local payments) has never been sold as a product.

There is also a lot of ready inventory: about twenty vertical "Studio" template sites in the Wix account (dental, clinic, restaurant, travel, construction, auto, pet care, agency, retail), built in May 2026. That is the "launch a business in 90 minutes" library from the 2025 notes, sitting unused.

## The decision

**Kurongeka is a productised launch-and-run service for small businesses. Kapture is the studio.**

- **Kurongeka** sells fixed-scope, fixed-price launches from proven business models, then recurring monthly care. Fast, repeatable, cheap to deliver.
- **Kapture** keeps bespoke and enterprise work (Air Zimbabwe, The Visual Bible, AWEF): high-touch, high-ticket.
- Kurongeka is endorsed: "Kurongeka, by Kapture". Kapture delivers the work behind it.

**Promise:** Your business, properly set up.
**Name:** Shona for "to be properly arranged". It is the whole brand story in one word.

### Who it is for

1. Owner-run businesses in Zimbabwe and the region, served remotely from the UK (clinics, salons, restaurants, tour operators, garages, contractors, churches, schools, retailers) that need to be found, look credible and get paid.
2. UK small businesses, and diaspora-owned businesses in the UK and investors setting up businesses at home. They pay in pounds and value reliability.

### Why this wins

- **It sells what already works.** Kapture has delivered dozens of these sites. Productising removes the long sales cycle that stalls the bespoke Brand Transformation Module.
- **Proven models cut delivery cost.** Starting from a model for the business type makes a launch a configuration job, not a design project.
- **Recurring revenue makes it a business.** Launches pay the bills; Run plans build value.
- **The directory becomes the marketplace later.** Every launched business can be listed (with consent). The "real businesses, real legal entities" marketplace from the 2025 notes grows from customers instead of being built before them.

### What we stop doing

- The ERP and "beat SAP" positioning. It needs capital and years; small businesses do not want an ERP.
- The £350 audit as a paid front door. It becomes the free Kurongeka Check (lead capture) and part of every launch.
- Kurongeka OS as a public product. Staff run the business from the Wix Dashboard.
- Patent claims and invented proof on the public site.

## The offer

Three steps: **Check** (free) → **Launch** (one-off) → **Run** (monthly). All prices in GBP.

### Launch packages (Wix CMS: LaunchPackages)

| Package | Price | Live in | Best for |
|---|---|---|---|
| Essentials | £390 | ~7 working days | New businesses and sole traders who need to look credible and be found |
| Pro (recommended) | £990 | ~14 working days | Trading businesses ready to take bookings, orders and payments |
| Complete | £1,650 | ~21 working days | Established businesses repositioning or pitching for tenders; matches the existing Brand Transformation Module |

Invoiced 50% to start, 50% at launch (Wix Invoices). The client owns the site, domain and data. The Wix premium plan is billed by Wix in the client's name, never marked up.

### Run plans (Wix Pricing Plans, monthly, cancel any time)

| Plan | Price | What it covers |
|---|---|---|
| Care | £39/month | Updates, security, monthly health report, 1 hour of changes, WhatsApp support |
| Grow | £119/month | Care + 8 posts, 1 article, Google Business posts, quarterly re-score, 3 hours of changes |
| Partner | £319/month | Grow + dedicated manager, 20 posts, quarterly campaign, monthly strategy call |

### Later (phase 2)

- **Kits:** self-serve digital products at £19–£49 (the Profile and Tender Kit, Personal Ops template) through Wix Stores. Low-ticket entry and email capture.
- **Directory:** verified listings of launched businesses. Page exists, empty until the first consented listing.
- **Kurongeka for Property:** Shopps and Konnect as a group offer for shopping centres and landlords (every tenant gets a launch at a group rate). Arundel Village is the pilot conversation.

## Unit economics (illustrative)

Assumptions to replace with real numbers: blended delivery cost £15 an hour; tools £20–£50 per launch.

| Product | Price | Hours | Direct cost | Gross margin |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Essentials | £390 | 10 | £170 | 56% |
| Pro | £990 | 20 | £330 | 67% |
| Complete | £1,650 | 35 | £575 | 65% |
| Run Care | £39/mo | 1 | £15 | 62% |
| Run Grow | £119/mo | 4 | £60 | 50% |
| Run Partner | £319/mo | 10 | £150 | 53% |

**Year-one scenario.** 3 launches a month for months 1–3, 6 a month for months 4–6, 10 a month for months 7–12 gives 87 launches. With a 40/45/15 mix of Essentials/Pro/Complete, the average launch is about £850, so roughly **£74k in launch revenue**. If half of launches take a Run plan averaging £79 a month with 3% monthly churn, that is about **40 Run clients and £3.2k MRR by month 12**. This is a model to steer by, not a forecast.

The number that matters most is **Run attach rate**. Every Run client is worth roughly £950 a year and makes the next launch cheaper to sell.

## Go to market

WhatsApp-first, because that is how the market buys.

1. **Warm network (weeks 1–6).** Kapture's past clients and contacts: 10 launches at full price, each with consent to be a case study and directory listing.
2. **Partners.** Business associations, accountants and company registration agents (CR14/CR6 and tender readiness), bank SME desks, shopping centres. A referral fee of 10% of the launch price.
3. **Content.** Instagram @kurongekadotcom, Facebook and LinkedIn: before-and-after launches, "Kurongeka in 60 seconds" per business type, the free check as the call to action.
4. **Diaspora.** UK community groups and churches; "set up the business at home properly".
5. **Paid test.** £250 on Meta targeting business owners in Harare and Bulawayo, sending to the check. Measure cost per check and per call before spending more.
